How this builds a lean, wide shape
The 3 rules
  • 1
    Wide shoulders: side delts are trained 3× a week (10 sets). They make the "V" more than anything else.
  • 2
    Wide back: lats are trained 3× a week with pull-ups, pulldowns and rows.
  • 3
    Small waist: comes from fat loss, not crunches. Skip heavy weighted side bends; they thicken the obliques.

Only 3 days this week?

Do Upper A, Lower A, Upper B. Add 2 sets of lateral raises and 3 sets of leg press to the end of Upper B.

Weekly check-in

Take front and side photos on Day 1, 30, 60 and 90 in the same light. Weigh in every morning after the toilet, before food, and compare weekly averages. Aim to lose 0.4–0.7 kg/week. If the average doesn't move for 2 weeks, cut rice at dinner to 20 g and drop the walnuts (about −150 kcal).

Allergy-safe

Fiber lands well above 30 g, which is normal on a plant-based diet. 30 g is the minimum. Drink your water and increase it if you feel bloated in week 1.

Sunday prep (makes "same food daily" easy)
  • 1
    Pre-portion 7 bags of oats (50 g) + nuts (15 g almonds, 10 g walnuts).
  • 2
    Pre-portion 7 bags of snack mix: 30 g roasted chana + 20 g peanuts + 10 g sunflower seeds.
  • 3
    Wash and chop vegetables for 3–4 days and store them in boxes.
  • 4
    Buy tofu every 3–4 days so it stays fresh. Weigh soya chunks dry (40 g).
  • 5
    Rotate the vegetables (bhindi, cabbage, beans, capsicum, spinach) so you don't get bored. The macros barely change.
